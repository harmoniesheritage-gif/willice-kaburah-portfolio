Biography & Passion

Inspiring Choirs Through Thoughtful Harmony & Precision

Willice Kaburah is a celebrated music educator, composer, arranger, and music engraver. With a deep passion for SATB choral counterpoint and African sacred song arrangements, his compositions combine rich vocal harmonies with uplifting thematic devotion.

As the visionary behind Heritage Harmonies-KE, Willice serves church choirs, educational institutions, and competitive vocal ensembles across the region by providing pristine sheet music engraving, vocal arranging, and masterclass coaching.
